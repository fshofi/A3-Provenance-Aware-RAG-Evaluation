# RAG Answer Evidence Review
Author: Shofi Ahmed Uddin

## Buyer problem
Trace generated claims to their supplied passages, expose evidence gaps, and give a reviewer a clear publication decision.

## Pilot contract
- Buyer: RAG builders, knowledge-product teams and technical content owners.
- Scope: Up to 20 supplied answers and their retrieved passages, from one bounded use case.
- Outputs: A claim-to-passage ledger, citation and provenance findings, a reviewer-adjudicated sample, and a prioritised repair memo.
- Inputs: supplied answers/data and reference documents; intended use; named accountable owner; agreed access route.
- Acceptance: every in-scope item is linked to evidence and a finding; every computed figure has a rerun route; missing inputs and unresolved judgments are explicit.
- Delivery: agree after a sample inspection. Scope or data changes require a revised agreement.
- Fee: fixed quotation after scoping; no automatic subscription.
- Exclusions: certification, legal opinion, production deployment, unrestricted security testing, guaranteed commercial outcomes.

## Evidence boundary
Human-reviewed service. Lexical similarity is not entailment. Source hashes check consistency of supplied records, not publisher authenticity or truth.
