# Limitations and prohibited claims

## Mandatory residual limitations

- Deterministic lexical support is not semantic entailment or truth verification.
- Sentence splitting is not complete proposition extraction.
- Contradiction patterns are bounded and may miss implicit conflict.
- Retrieval omission can be tested only when required evidence is declared.
- Source authority is metadata, not an independently certified fact.
- The reference corpus is purposive and cannot establish population performance.
- No production vector store, LLM, external tool or client data is connected.
- No independent human validation has been completed.

## Prohibited claims

A3 must not be described as a production RAG platform, universal hallucination detector, truth engine, legal-compliance certification, fairness certification, security audit, or independently validated client system.
