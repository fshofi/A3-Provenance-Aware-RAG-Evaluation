Visible limitations

  • V1 uses deterministic lexical support rather than neural entailment.
  • Contradiction rules are deliberately bounded and cannot certify truth.
  • Publisher and authority metadata require independent verification in operational use.
  • No vector database, production RAG system or external action is connected.
  • No independent human validation or certification claim is made.
