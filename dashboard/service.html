RAG Answer Evidence Review

Trace generated claims to their supplied passages, expose evidence gaps, and give a reviewer a clear publication decision.

One bounded engagement

Up to 20 supplied answers and their retrieved passages, from one bounded use case.

Deliverables

A claim-to-passage ledger, citation and provenance findings, a reviewer-adjudicated sample, and a prioritised repair memo.

Acceptance

Every in-scope item has an evidence link, a finding and a clear statement of what remains unresolved. Computed figures have a reproducible check.

What to bring

A small sample, your intended use, the relevant source material or scores, and a named accountable owner. Access and handling are agreed before confidential material is shared.

Timing and fee

Fixed scope, fee and delivery date agreed after inspecting a sample. No subscription required.

Sample deliverable

The challenge set exposes false support from negation, changed entities and added properties. Conservative screening routes non-exact support to a reviewer.
