# Governance contract

## Core rules

1. Retrieval does not establish evidence quality.
2. A syntactically valid citation does not establish claim support.
3. Source authority must be proportionate to consequence.
4. Evidence must remain valid at the evaluation time.
5. Evaluation completion does not create publication authority.
6. Human approval is necessary in defined cases but never repairs defective evidence.
7. Every material transition must be reconstructable from the output audit.

## Authority rule

V1 records `publication_authorised=true` only when all of the following hold:

- the decision is `SUPPORTED`;
- the declared consequence is `low`;
- explicit human approval is recorded.

No external publishing or operational action is implemented.
